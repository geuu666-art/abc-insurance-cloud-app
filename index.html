ABC Insurance Digital Claims and Document Routing Service

This cloud-based application confirms that ABC Insurance SDN BHD has successfully deployed a basic cloud service for its digital transformation.

The service supports insurance and logistics-related operations, including medical and vehicle claim evidence submission, signed policy digitisation, physical mail sorting, and document routing across multiple branches.

Supported Operations

  • Medical and vehicle claim evidence upload
  • Signed policy digitisation
  • Physical mail sorting and document routing
  • Procurement and stock tracking support
  • Multi-branch access for 24-hour operations
